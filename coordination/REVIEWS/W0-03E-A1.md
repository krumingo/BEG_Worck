### Blocking authorization defect: unowned `project_team` membership

`backend/app/tenancy/data_access.py:315-342` explicitly documents that legacy `project_team` rows carry no tenant key. Nevertheless, `assigned_project_ids()` queries them by `user_id` and `active` only; `is_project_member()` queries them by `project_id`, `user_id`, `active` and optional role only. Looking up an A-owned project **after** reading an unowned membership row does not establish who created that row. These helpers are used in the protected finance invoice/list/detail and offer authorization paths (`finance.py:621,681,697,837`; `offers.py:41,49,179`). The inventory marks those joins `SAFE` (`docs/architecture/W0-03E-A1_INVENTORY.md:47,49,57` and other offer rows), but their membership provenance is unproved.

Codex ran an independent deterministic A/B collision reproduction directly against the reviewed `data_access.py`, with an in-memory async collection stand-in and no runtime-code edits. Projects `{id:p1, org_id:A}` and `{id:p1, org_id:B}` both exist. The **only** team row is `{project_id:p1, user_id:u1, active:true, role_in_project:SiteManager, origin:B-only}`. For a server-resolved A user `u1`, `assigned_project_ids()` returned `['p1']` and `is_project_member(..., 'p1', 'SiteManager')` returned `True`. Thus a B-only team row authorizes A access to an A project to which A's user was never assigned. This violates the requested fail-closed tenant-bound access contract even though the later project document itself belongs to A. The existing unit test (`backend/tests/test_w0_03e_a1_tenant_access.py:147-156`) covers a B-only **different** project ID, not simultaneous user/project ID collision.

The correct provenance of ownerless legacy team rows cannot be inferred from matching IDs. Do not silently guess a tenant, call the central layer complete, or assume that the separate-database deployment model makes this shared-legacy-DB case irrelevant. It needs a proven tenant-bound source or a separate authorized migration/architecture decision; this review does not make that decision.
