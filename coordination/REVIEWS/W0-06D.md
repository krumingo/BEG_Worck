### Bounded defects

1. **False readiness without original/checksum proof.** In `adoption_readiness.py:_classify`, the `READY_TO_ADOPT` candidate is only `registry["verified_location"]`; `propose_action` then yields `ADOPT_IN_PLACE`. Independently executed the pure classifier with a same-tenant registered File whose location has stored `availability=available`, but `observed.state=unknown`, `checksum=None`, `size_bytes=None`, `checksum_matches=None` and zero relations. Actual result: `READY_TO_ADOPT [] ADOPT_IN_PLACE`. W0-06D contract requires proven original, checksum/size, business ownership and customer-managed location. A stored location status is not proof of this legacy original. Such rows must remain blocked/decision-required until evidence is complete. `READY_TO_ADOPT` must not be the fallback for a registry location alone.
2. **Unrelated relation accepted as already registered.** `_registry_state` reduces all FileRelations to `relation_count`; `_classify` calls `ALREADY_REGISTERED` when that count is nonzero, the checksum matches and a location is marked available. It never compares the proposed relation type/target from `plan.relations` to the actual same-tenant relations, nor verifies that target. Independent pure counterexample: plan relation `project=missing-project`, registry has one unrelated relation, matching checksum and available location; actual result `ALREADY_REGISTERED []`. The contract requires an exact File/FileVersion/location/**relation** match and blocked ambiguous targets. Resolve and compare exact relation targets before `ALREADY_REGISTERED` or `REGISTER_REFERENCE_ONLY`; a count cannot substitute for identity.
3. **Mutable scan budget makes same-input validation stale.** `LegacyRootInventory` stores `checksums_read`, `bytes_read` and `objects_seen` on the reusable object. `scan()` calls `walk()` and `observe_path()`; `validate_plan()` reuses the same service/inventory and calls `scan(..., persist=False)` without resetting a per-pass budget. Independent same-input call to `_checksum` with a one-object limit returned the checksum first, then `CHECKSUM_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` on the second call. At or near a budget limit, a fresh scan/validation can therefore change fingerprints with no DB, original, provider or relation drift and falsely raise `AdoptionPlanStale`. Reset/account budget per scan pass and test repeated validation at exact limits (including a DB row and physical walk sharing one object).
4. **Actual content read can exceed the declared byte cap.** `_checksum` checks the pre-read `size` and remaining byte budget once, then reads until EOF without checking each chunk. If the file grows after `stat`, or an opener returns more than the reported size, it reads and hashes beyond both `max_object_bytes` and `max_checksum_bytes`. Independent injected-stream counterexample: reported size and both caps `1`, stream `b"abc"`; result was a successful checksum and `bytes_read=3`. The contract requires a bounded content read. Enforce both caps during streaming and fail closed on overrun; add a growing-file/injected-stream regression.
