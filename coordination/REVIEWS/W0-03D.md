## Finding — history loses causal order when timestamps tie

`backend/app/master_data/merge.py:361` sorts history by `(recorded_at, id)`. `_now()` at line 217 can return the same timestamp for consecutive merge and unmerge events on Windows. Their IDs are not a causal sequence; sorting by ID can put the correction before the merge it reverses. This contradicts the immutable, intelligible merge/correction history required by `docs/architecture/W0-03_MASTER_DATA_INVENTORY_AND_CONTRACT.md` §W0-03D and `docs/flows/FLOW-032.md` and breaks the existing focused test.
